A patch release of the open firmware and MCP server for the FNIRSI 1013D (Anlogic AL3A10 variant): two follow-ups to
0.4.1. It needs the same FPGA bitstream as 0.3.0 to 0.4.1, **0x153F**, so you only update the firmware on the card.
Your calibration stays valid.

Documentation: <https://mikljohansson.github.io/fnirsi-1013d-mcp/> (install guide, the screens, the agent guide). The
0.3.0 notes describe everything this firmware does: <https://github.com/mikljohansson/fnirsi-1013d-mcp/releases/tag/v0.3.0>.

### What changed

- **The trigger level is right at 500 mV/div (10X) with a DC offset.** On the most sensitive range (50 mV/div at the
  input, shown as 500 mV/div with a 10X probe), a channel with a DC offset set had its trigger level placed wrongly:
  the scope triggered at a slightly different voltage than the T marker showed. On a fast edge at 10 ns/div that left
  the trigger point about half a sample (2 ns) beside the H marker, which is what remained after 0.4.1. The level now
  follows the marker. Thanks to andrewpono for checking 0.4.1 and reporting back
  (<https://github.com/mikljohansson/fnirsi-1013d-mcp/issues/3>).
- **The expanded measurement cards stay expanded.** If you tap the cards to show every selected measurement, the
  scope now remembers that, also after power-off, until you fold them again. So you can have everything you selected
  on the screen all the time, or keep the single row. This follows a suggestion by andrewpono
  (<https://github.com/mikljohansson/fnirsi-1013d-mcp/issues/4>).

### Update from 0.3.0 to 0.4.1

1. Download `fnirsi_1013d-fw-0.4.2.bin` and `SHA256SUMS`, and check: `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS --ignore-missing`.
2. Write the firmware to the card. With the MCP package: `fnirsi1013d-flash --image fnirsi_1013d-fw-0.4.2.bin
   --sha256 <hash> --persist` (it backs up the sectors it replaces). Or build the SD parts and write only the scope part
   with the SD tools, as the install guide describes. The bitstream stays 0x153F: no programmer needed.
3. Coming from 0.3.0, run the calibration wizard for 0.3.1's zero correction: both probes unplugged from the inputs and
   USB unplugged for the zero step, then each probe at x1 on the calibration post. From 0.3.1 or later nothing is needed.
4. MCP: the server is unchanged since 0.4.1; install the attached wheel if you are on an older one.

A new install follows the 0.3.0 steps with the 0.4.2 firmware file: <https://mikljohansson.github.io/fnirsi-1013d-mcp/guide/install/>.

### Known issues

- **A few pixels can remain at 10 ns/div.** Where the edge is shallow at the trigger level (near its top or bottom),
  the trigger point can sit some pixels from the H marker; on the steep part it is within about 1 ns.
- **A tap just beside a measurement card still goes to the plot** and can change V/div or the time base. Taps on a
  card do not.
- **Bars and peak-detect traces, and saved waveforms, still start on a whole sample.**
- **Spectrum and Frequency response: "+N more" is still only a count** there.
- **Probe ground is not USB ground.** The probe ground clips sit on an internal reference about 1.24 V above the
  scope's USB ground. If the device you measure shares the PC's USB ground and a ground clip is on it, every trace
  shifts by about -1.2 V. Power the device from a battery or through a USB isolator, or leave the clip off for slow
  signals (`docs/agent-measurement-guide.md`).
- **One unit.** Everything was benched on a single AL3A10 unit.
- The other items listed for 0.4.1, 0.4.0, 0.3.4, 0.3.3 and 0.3.1 are unchanged:
  <https://github.com/mikljohansson/fnirsi-1013d-mcp/releases/tag/v0.4.1>.
- **The firmware runs only with bitstream 0x153F**; other bitstreams show a banner.
